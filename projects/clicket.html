Overview

Clicket is a smart camera accessory system, a clip-on device, a companion app and a rental service that helps new photographers practise outdoors. Instead of adding more features to the camera, it brings back what actually makes people improve: going out, counting shots, and learning from people who have been there.

Satisfaction index (S.I.)

Device difficulty

1

Starter Input

The Newbies start with far less preparation than the Pros.

2

Learning Process

The Pros take in much more feedback while learning; the Newbies take in little.

3

Learning Consistency

The Newbies’ fluency is patchy, with frequent gaps. The Pros show almost none.

4

Device Switching

The Pros switch devices once their fluency is ready. The Newbies switch before it is.

5

Device Mismatch

Fluency lags behind device difficulty, so the Newbies’ S.I. drops sharply whenever a new device arrives.
